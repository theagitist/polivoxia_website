    About me

    The person behind the projects
    Adri M. · they / them / any

    I make interactive works, games, zines and apps, where art, computers and community meet.

    I'm a master's student at the UBC Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice (GRSJ). My research centres on JOURNEYWAYS, a board game and a digital game used as research instruments to study how people self-identify in a narrative-driven, safe, collaborative play space.

    The project was awarded the Canada Graduate Research Scholarship: Master's (CGS-M) in April 2026, and was selected for UBC's Critical Play Fellowship in September 2025.

    Positionality

    I am a trans/nonbinary refugee from the land of the Huetar people (so-called Costa Rica), now living in Vancouver. I navigate multiple borders, geographic, cultural and gendered, and those crossings shape the transdisciplinary, decolonial perspective I bring to my work.

    My commitment to social justice is personal. It centres the lived experiences of equity-seeking communities, particularly forced migrants and gender-diverse people, and it is held in community: I have organized with the Vancouver Trans March and facilitated peer support for trans, nonbinary and gender-diverse people. Through research, activism and storytelling, I aim to amplify voices often left at the margins and to imagine futures grounded in joy, dignity and collective care.
