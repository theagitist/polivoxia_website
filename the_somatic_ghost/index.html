About the Zine

This zine maps the fourteen-day rhapsody of a FOLFOXIRI chemotherapy cycle: from liquid fire to first light. It is a tool for navigating poisonous knowledge, a refusal of patience, and a record of the 1% response.

The Somatic Ghost is a counter-archive: a transition from being a Patient (one who suffers and waits) to being an Author who acts and documents. Day by day, it charts the infusion, the crash, the purge, and ultimately the awesome window where the body reclaims its own rhythm.

I am not a patient. I am an author. And I am the archive.
